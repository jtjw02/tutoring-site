How it works — preview

Four steps, no guesswork.

#1Understand

First principles.

Every topic starts with the concept itself, taught from first principles, so it actually makes sense rather than just being memorised. Rote learning gets partway there, but caps how far marks can really go.

#2Diagnose

Find the gap.

Exercises, tests, and homework reveal exactly where marks are still slipping — a specific technique, a formula, or a step that isn't automatic yet — not a vague sense of needing more practice.

#3Rebuild

Fix it properly.

Whatever that practice turns up gets rebuilt from wherever it actually broke down, not from the start of the textbook, so time goes toward what's missing rather than what's already solid.

#4Automate

Exam conditions.

Closer to SACs and exams, sessions shift to timed practice marked against VCAA criteria, so the process holds up under real exam pressure, not just in a quiet room with no clock.

#5Resources

Notes included.

Session notes, printed materials, and recordings of online sessions, so what happens in the lesson carries into the week between sessions.

#6Support

Between sessions.

One-to-one attention doesn't stop at the lesson. Questions get answered between sessions, and rescheduling is free — no commitment, one-off sessions available.
