Tutoring

How it works.

What an actual session looks like, from the first diagnostic through to exam day.

Taught properly, then sharpened by practice.

Sessions cover VCE General Maths content properly and structurally, understanding each concept from first principles rather than rushing to the formula. From there, exercises and practice work reveal exactly where marks are still being lost, and sessions target that specific gap until it holds up under real exam conditions.

Sessions run one-on-one, online or in person, for around 60 minutes, sometimes extending to 90 if that week's content needs it.

The process
UNDERSTAND
The concept

Structured lessons cover the content properly, from first principles, understanding what a formula or method actually represents rather than just how to plug numbers into it. Rote memorisation gets partway there, but without this layer, it caps how far marks can really go.

DIAGNOSE
The read

Once the concept has been taught properly, exercises, tests, and homework reveal exactly where marks are still being lost, a specific technique, a formula, or a step that isn't quite automatic yet.

REBUILD
The build

Sessions then rebuild that specific gap from wherever it actually broke down, not from the start of the textbook, so time goes toward what's missing rather than what's already solid.

AUTOMATE
The finish

Closer to SACs and exams, sessions shift to timed practice marked against VCAA criteria, so the process holds up under actual exam pressure, not just in a quiet room with no clock running.
